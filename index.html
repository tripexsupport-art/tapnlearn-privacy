Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

TapnLearn is a vocabulary learning app designed for young children. This policy describes what information the app handles and how it is used. We have written it to be readable by parents and guardians — not just lawyers.

1 · Information We Collect

Stored only on your device — never sent to our servers:

  • Your language selection and app settings (labels, speech, quick mode, spelling toggle)
  • Learning progress, quiz scores, category stars, streaks, and sticker collection
  • Anonymous event logs (for example: "quiz completed", "category opened") tagged with a randomly-generated install ID. No name, email address, or personally identifiable information is ever attached.

When you subscribe to TapnLearn Premium:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment card details.
  • Your subscription status is cached locally on your device to avoid repeated network calls.

2 · What We Do Not Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or any contact information
  • Persistent device identifiers that could identify an individual across apps
  • Location data of any kind
  • Photos, camera content, or images from your device
  • Microphone recordings — the optional speech-practice feature processes audio entirely on your device using the operating system's built-in speech recognition; no audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted
  • Any personal information from children

3 · Advertising

TapnLearn displays advertisements through Google AdMob. All ad requests from this app are sent with child-directed treatment flags enabled, as required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Families Ads Programme. This disables interest-based targeting, remarketing, and personalised advertising.

Ads appear in the Parents section of the app (behind a tap-hold parental gate) as a way to earn free bonus content. They are not targeted at children based on their behaviour.

4 · Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

TapnLearn is designed for use by young children under adult supervision. We do not knowingly collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information from any person under the age of 13.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us using the details in Section 7 and we will delete it within 30 days of your request.

5 · Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services. Each is governed by its own privacy policy, linked below:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (in-app subscriptions) — policies.google.com/privacy

6 · Data Security

All learning data and preferences are stored exclusively on your device using the standard Android SharedPreferences API. We do not operate servers that store your personal data. The only network activity initiated by the app is ad requests and purchase transactions, both described above.

7 · Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL. Material changes will be noted by updating the date at the top of the page.
