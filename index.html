Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

TapnLearn is a vocabulary learning app designed for young children. This policy describes what information the app handles and how it is used. We have written it to be readable by parents and guardians — not just lawyers.

1 · Information We Collect

Stored locally by TapnLearn:

  • Your language selection and app settings (labels, speech, quick mode, spelling toggle)
  • Learning progress, quiz scores, category stars, streaks, and sticker collection
  • Event logs (for example, "quiz completed" or "category opened") and a randomly generated installation identifier. The app stores these locally and does not upload them to our servers.

Subscriptions and service information:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment card details.
  • TapnLearn uses RevenueCat to retrieve available subscription options, check access, and restore purchases. RevenueCat generates an anonymous app user ID and processes subscription and transaction information. Its service may also receive technical information such as an IP address and device information. Subscription status may be cached on the device.
  • Google AdMob may receive technical and usage information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or app identifiers when its SDK is used. IP addresses can indicate an approximate location.

2 · Microphone and Device Content

Optional pronunciation practice requests microphone permission when enabled. TapnLearn requests on-device speech recognition where supported and does not save the audio as a recording. The available recognition service depends on the device. Some services may process or transmit audio, so review your device's speech-recognition provider's privacy information.

TapnLearn does not request access to your contacts, photos, or camera for its learning features.

3 · Advertising

TapnLearn displays advertisements through Google AdMob to users without Premium. Ad requests are configured for child-directed treatment with a G maximum content rating. The release app removes the Android advertising ID permissions.

Banner ads may appear on the home screen. Optional rewarded ads for a bonus sticker or streak recovery require a grown-up check. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

4 · Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

TapnLearn is designed for use by young children under adult supervision. Its third-party services may process the technical and subscription information described above. Parents can contact us about information associated with a child's use of the app.

If you have questions about a child's information, or want to request its deletion, contact us using the details below.

5 · Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services. Each is governed by its own privacy policy, linked below:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (in-app subscriptions) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management and verification) — revenuecat.com/privacy-policy

6 · Data Security

Learning progress and preferences are stored locally using Android's app storage. The app uses network services for advertising and subscription management as described above. The device's speech-recognition service may also use a network connection.

7 · Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL. Material changes will be noted by updating the date at the top of the page.

Contact & Deletion Requests

To remove locally stored learning progress, settings, and event logs, clear TapnLearn's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. A device backup may retain a copy. Clearing app storage does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion of information held by our service providers, email us. Describe your request and the app involved, without sending a child's sensitive information. We may need information to locate the relevant service record. Google Play and our service providers may retain records under their own policies or applicable legal obligations.
